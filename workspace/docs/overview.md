# Workspace Overview

The **Workspace** is the central hub for user interaction in AgentWorX. It's where you bring together your private documents, your AI agents, and your team, and interact with all of it through a single chat interface.

If you're new to AgentWorX, think of a workspace as your project's home base: everything you upload, every agent you talk to, and every conversation you have is organized within it.

## Key Features

- **Chat interface** for LLM interactions — talk directly to an AI agent about the content and context of your workspace.
- **Thread management** to keep conversations organized — every distinct topic or task can live in its own thread, instead of one long, mixed conversation.
- **Document grounding** context for the LLMs — documents you upload are used to ground the AI's answers in your actual data, rather than relying on general knowledge alone.

## How a Workspace Fits Together

A workspace connects several other parts of AgentWorX:

| Component | Role within the workspace |
|---|---|
| **Documents** | The private data the AI uses to answer questions ([Document Upload](./document-upload.md)) |
| **Agents** | The AI personas that respond in chat, each with their own skills and configuration |
| **Workflows** | Automated multi-step processes the workspace can trigger |
| **Users** | The people with access to the workspace, and their roles (Admin/Member) |
| **Settings** | Workspace-level overrides for LLM, vector database, and appearance ([Workspace Settings](./workspace-settings.md)) |
